Model notes

How the rankings work.

Week 0 is a forward-looking roster score. Weekly performance joins the model after games are played.

01

55% · Starting lineup

The highest projected legal Week 1 lineup: QB, RB, WR, TE, three FLEX, D/ST, and kicker. This is the forecast’s primary input.

02

25% · Star power

The top three projected RB, WR, and TE players raise a team’s ceiling and receive more influence than interchangeable bench depth.

03

5% · Skill depth

The best remaining RB, WR, and TE options provide a small durability adjustment without overstating bench value.

04

5% · Injury risk

Questionable, doubtful, out, and injured-reserve designations reduce the projection according to the affected player’s expected role.

05

10% · Wild card

Low-owned players with meaningful projections, injury-created opportunity, and high-upside stashes create a controlled upside adjustment.

06

Projected points ± volatility

The raw optimized-lineup projection is the baseline. The other four factors make limited point adjustments around it—there is no preset league average or artificial score curve.

After every week, projected points will be compared with actual points. That forecast error will be used to recalibrate the model without changing the meaning of the published score.
