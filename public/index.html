Week 0 is a forward-looking roster score. Weekly performance joins the model after games are played.

01

The highest projected legal lineup: QB, RB, WR, TE, three FLEX, D/ST, and kicker. ESPN’s 2026 season projections supply the player values.

20% · Skill depth

The best remaining RB, WR, and TE options after the optimized lineup is filled. This rewards usable injury and bye-week coverage.

03

15% · Star power

The top three projected RB, WR, and TE players on each roster. This captures the weekly ceiling created by elite players.

04

Score scale

Each component is ranked against the other 13 teams. The blended percentile is translated to a 70–98 score; it is a relative league rating, not a projected point total.
